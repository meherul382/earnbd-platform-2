Four Things to Check Before Buying

  1. Confirm which RAM and storage variant the listed price refers to.
  2. Check whether local or official warranty is included.
  3. Ask whether taxes, delivery or other charges are separate.
  4. Confirm the current price and stock with the seller on the day you buy.
