Women’s Hospitals in the United States: Major Centers to Know

Women’s health programs in the United States provide services across areas such as obstetrics, gynecology, breast health and reproductive care. This guide is for general research and is not a ranking.

Major U.S. centers to research

1. Brigham and Women’s Hospital — Boston, Massachusetts

A major academic hospital with women’s health services.

2. Mayo Clinic — Arizona, Florida and Minnesota

A large nonprofit medical organization with women’s health and specialty services.

3. Cleveland Clinic — Ohio and other U.S. locations

A large health system offering multiple specialty services.

4. Northwestern Medicine Prentice Women’s Hospital — Chicago, Illinois

A dedicated women’s hospital within Northwestern Medicine.

5. Johns Hopkins Hospital — Baltimore, Maryland

An academic medical center with specialty and women’s health services.

6. UCLA Health — Los Angeles, California

A major academic health system with women’s health services.

7. NYU Langone Health — New York, New York

A large academic health system with women’s health programs.

8. Mount Sinai — New York, New York

A large medical system with obstetrics, gynecology and related specialty care.

9. Cedars-Sinai — Los Angeles, California

A major medical center with women’s health services.

10. Stanford Health Care — Stanford, California

An academic health system with specialty and women’s health care.

What to compare

  • Exact service or specialty needed
  • Insurance and referral requirements
  • Location and travel time
  • Appointment availability
  • Hospital and clinician credentials

Medical information note

This page is general educational information, not diagnosis or medical advice. Confirm current services, locations, insurance participation and appointment requirements directly with the provider. For emergencies, call 911.
