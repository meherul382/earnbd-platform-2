Safer Everyday Technology Habits

  • Check the developer, permissions and reviews before installing an app.
  • Keep your operating system and important apps updated.
  • Do not enter sensitive information on unfamiliar websites or download pages.
  • Verify AI-generated information before using it for important decisions.
  • Use unique passwords and strong account authentication wherever possible.
