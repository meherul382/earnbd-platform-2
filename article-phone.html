What to Do When Your Phone Becomes Slow

Storage management, app maintenance and software updates can help keep a phone responsive over time.

Check Storage

When storage is nearly full, managing updates, caches and new files can become harder. Review large videos, duplicate downloads and unused apps.

Review Apps and Updates

  • Uninstall apps you no longer use.
  • Reduce unnecessary background activity.
  • Install trusted system and app security updates.
  • Check the developer and permissions before installing unfamiliar cleaner or booster apps.

When Should You Seek Service?

Back up important data before a factory reset or repair. If the battery is swollen, the phone becomes unusually hot or it repeatedly shuts down, avoid opening it yourself and contact an authorized service provider.

Final Tip

Regular storage management, updates and careful app selection can help a phone remain useful for longer.
