How to Read Mobile News Responsibly

  • Prefer official announcements for confirmed specifications and release information.
  • Treat leaks, rumors and predictions as unconfirmed until independently verified.
  • Check the date, market, storage variant and warranty when reading a price.
  • Verify current price and after-sales support before making a purchase.
