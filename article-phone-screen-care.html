PHONE CARE GUIDE

How to Protect and Maintain a Phone Screen

A phone screen is exposed to dust, fingerprints, drops, pressure and heat every day. Careful habits can reduce avoidable damage and keep the display comfortable to use. Protection should be practical rather than based on unnecessary accessories.

Choose a suitable case

A case with a raised edge around the display can provide some protection when the phone is placed face down or experiences a small impact. Make sure the case does not press against the screen or block microphones, speakers and charging ports.

Use a compatible screen protector

A quality protector may help reduce scratches and minor surface damage. Check that it fits the phone model and does not interfere with touch sensitivity, fingerprint sensors or the front camera. No protector can guarantee that a screen will survive a serious drop.

Clean the screen gently

Use a soft, lint-free cloth. If needed, slightly dampen the cloth with water according to the manufacturer’s instructions, and never pour liquid directly onto the phone. Avoid abrasive powders, sharp tools and household cleaners that may damage coatings.

Avoid pressure and extreme heat

Do not place heavy objects on the phone or keep it under a pillow while charging. High heat can affect the battery and other components. If the screen separates from the frame or the device becomes unusually hot, stop using it and seek professional inspection.

Handle cracks carefully

A cracked screen can expose sharp edges and may allow moisture or dust to enter. Do not press repeatedly on damaged glass. Back up your data and ask a reputable technician about repair options, part quality and whether the repair affects water resistance.

Quick checklist

  • Use a well-fitted protective case.
  • Choose a compatible screen protector.
  • Clean with a soft lint-free cloth.
  • Keep the phone away from excessive heat and pressure.
  • Back up data before screen repair.

Bottom line: Regular cleaning, sensible protection and careful handling can extend the useful life of a phone screen. Replace damaged accessories before they create a new problem.
