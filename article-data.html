7 Ways to Reduce Mobile Data Usage

If your mobile data runs out quickly, first identify which apps use the most data. Then adjust video, background activity and automatic-download settings.

1. Check Data Usage

Use your phone's network or data-usage settings to identify the apps consuming the most mobile data.

2. Limit Background Data

For apps that do not need constant background syncing, consider limiting background data when your device provides that option.

3. Reduce Video Quality

Streaming at a lower quality over mobile networks can reduce data consumption. Save large downloads for a trusted Wi-Fi connection when practical.

4. Control Automatic Downloads

Review automatic media downloads in messaging and social apps. Avoid downloading large files over mobile data unless necessary.

5. Review App Update Settings

Check whether your app store is allowed to update apps over mobile data. A Wi-Fi-only option may help on limited plans.

6. Use Offline Features

Where a service officially supports offline downloads, prepare content over Wi-Fi for later use.

7. Set a Data Warning

Many Android phones provide data-warning or limit settings. Configure them according to your mobile plan.

Final Tip

The most effective approach is to review usage regularly and adjust app settings according to your actual needs.
