AI and Productivity Guide

AI can help with research, brainstorming, drafting and repetitive work, but useful results require clear instructions and human review.

1. Write Clear Prompts

State your goal, context, desired output format and important constraints. Specific prompts usually produce more useful results than vague requests.

2. Verify Important Output

Do not treat AI-generated information as automatically correct. Check important claims, especially information about prices, laws, finance, health or current events.

3. Protect Sensitive Information

Avoid entering passwords, payment information, private documents or unnecessary personal information into an AI service. Review its privacy controls before using it for sensitive work.

4. Use AI as an Assistant

AI can help create outlines, brainstorm ideas, explain concepts and improve drafts. Keep your own judgment, fact checking and final editing in the workflow.

5. Build Reusable Workflows

For repetitive tasks, create reusable prompt templates and checklists. Review outputs before using them in important decisions.

Final Tip

The most productive AI workflow combines clear instructions, verification and human judgment rather than blindly accepting the first answer.
