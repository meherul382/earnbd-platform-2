5G Buying Guide

What to Check Before Buying a 5G Phone

Do not choose a 5G phone simply because it has a 5G logo. Check network availability, supported bands and modem compatibility, along with performance, battery life and software support.

Network bands and modem

Check whether the phone supports the 5G bands used by your mobile operator. The same phone name can have different network support depending on the region or model.

Processor and performance

Consider both the 5G modem and the chipset's CPU, GPU and efficiency. Your needs will differ if you mainly browse the web compared with gaming or video editing.

Battery and charging

Power consumption can vary with network conditions. Compare battery capacity with real-world battery results and supported charging speeds.

Camera and display

For cameras, consider the sensor, stabilization and video features. For displays, compare brightness, resolution and refresh rate rather than relying on one specification.

Price and warranty

When buying in Bangladesh, check the official warranty, purchase invoice, seller reputation and available after-sales service.

FAQ

Do all 5G phones work on the same networks?
No. Supported bands and operator compatibility can vary by model and region.

Does having 5G always make a phone faster?
No. Actual speed also depends on signal quality, network congestion, bands, the operator and your location.
