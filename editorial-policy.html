Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Our Editorial Approach

BanglaHub aims to publish clear, useful and original English technology content. We focus on practical explanations, context and real-world trade-offs rather than keyword-heavy specification lists.

Research and Fact Checking

We make reasonable efforts to compare reliable information before publishing or updating an article. Official manufacturer information is preferred for product specifications and announcements. Unconfirmed reports are not intentionally presented as confirmed facts.

Price Information

Prices can vary by country, seller, storage variant, warranty, taxes and stock. When a price is included, readers should treat it as time-sensitive and verify the current price before purchasing.

Reviews and Comparisons

Reviews and comparisons consider specifications together with practical factors such as performance, display, camera, battery, software support and value. Recommendations can differ according to budget and individual needs.

Originality

We aim to create our own explanations, summaries, comparisons and editorial context. We do not intentionally copy another publisher's article word for word.

Corrections

Readers can report factual errors, outdated information or broken links through our Contact page. Reported issues are reviewed and corrected when verification supports a change.

Advertising and Independence

Advertising does not determine our editorial assessments or correction decisions. Advertisements are not intended to be presented as independent editorial recommendations.

Contact

For editorial feedback or corrections, please use our Contact page.
