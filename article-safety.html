Important Rules for Staying Safe Online

Good password habits, strong authentication and careful handling of suspicious messages can significantly improve everyday account security.

Protect Your Accounts

  • Use a unique, strong password for every important account.
  • Enable two-factor authentication whenever it is available.
  • Review unfamiliar login sessions and security alerts.
  • Keep recovery email addresses and phone numbers up to date.

Watch for Phishing

Before opening an unfamiliar link or attachment, check the sender and destination domain. If a message urgently requests money, a password or a verification code, open the official app or website yourself instead of following the message link.

Review App Permissions

Give apps only the permissions they need for their purpose. Periodically review old third-party connections and remove access you no longer need.

Final Tip

Security works best as a routine: use unique passwords, enable strong authentication and pause before trusting unexpected links or requests.
